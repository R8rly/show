## What SHOW Is

SHOW is a four-axis content classification standard for narrative and creative works. It applies to any finished work regardless of how it was created — written by hand, collaboratively authored, AI-assisted, or fully generated. SHOW rates what the content contains. It is a reader and audience service, a platform tool, and a publishing standard.

SHOW is independent of production method. A human author and an AI-assisted author rate their finished work on identical terms. The standard classifies the work. It carries no implication about the creative process that produced it.

**S · H · O · W**

- **S — Spice** Physical explicitness — on-page or on-screen intimacy and detail
- **H — Heat** Emotional temperature — longing, romantic intensity, intimacy of soul
- **O — OMG** Darkness of tone — heaviness, peril, grief, violence, psychological weight
- **W — WTF** Transgression — taboo dynamics, wildness of content choices

Each axis is fully independent. High scores on one axis carry zero implication for any other axis.

## The Four Axes

### S — Spice (0–6)

*Physical explicitness — on-page or on-screen intimacy and detail.*

Spice classifies the explicitness of sexual content. Volume and frequency inform the upper levels; specific content types are communicated through content tags.

| Level | Label | Content present |
| :-: | :-: | :-- |
| 0 | Untouched | Kissing only. Door fully closed. |
| 1 | Suggestive | Fade-to-black, implied intimacy. |
| 2 | Open-Door | On-page intimacy with discretion. Sensation over anatomy. |
| 3 | Explicit | Anatomically clear, multiple scenes. |
| 4 | Carnal | High-frequency explicit scenes. Intimacy is load-bearing. |
| 5 | Hardcore | Kink, BDSM, power exchange at standard intensity. |
| 6 | Saturated | Explicit intimacy is the dominant content. |

### H — Heat (0–5)

*Emotional temperature — longing, romantic intensity, intimacy of soul.*

Heat measures the temperature of romantic and emotional tension — fully independent of physical content. A story with zero Spice can carry maximum Heat. A graphic erotic novel can carry low Heat if the emotional register is cool and transactional.

| Level | Label | Content present |
| :-: | :-: | :-- |
| 0 | Cool | Romance incidental or absent. |
| 1 | Warm | Affection, light flirtation. |
| 2 | Glowing | Attraction acknowledged. Lingering looks, first kisses. |
| 3 | Burning | Slow-burn intensity. Yearning. |
| 4 | Blazing | Vulnerable confessions, soul-bared scenes. |
| 5 | Wildfire | The relationship is the spine of the story. |

### O — OMG (0–5)

*Darkness of tone — heaviness, peril, grief, violence, psychological weight.*

OMG classifies what is emotionally and psychologically difficult in a work — the darkness a reader will be asked to sit with. OMG is fully independent of Spice. A grief memoir with no sexual content can carry OMG 5. An explicit erotic novel can carry OMG 0.

| Level | Label | Content present |
| :-: | :-: | :-- |
| 0 | Light | Sweet, cozy, comedy, family. |
| 1 | Shaded | Mild angst, mild peril. |
| 2 | Dim | On-page violence, dark themes with restraint. |
| 3 | Dark | Captivity, stalker, trauma, MC/mafia, war. |
| 4 | Black | Transgressive, dub-con, brutality. |
| 5 | Abyssal | Extreme — content-warning gated. |

### W — WTF (0–10)

*Transgression — taboo dynamics, wildness of content choices.*

WTF measures how far a work pushes beyond reader expectations and genre conventions. High WTF scores function as a reader service — they signal that the work operates in territory where standard assumptions about narrative, morality, and resolution are suspended.

| Level | Label | Content present |
| :-: | :-: | :-- |
| 0–2 | Vanilla | Conventional, no taboo. |
| 3–4 | Edgy | Mild transgression, age gap. |
| 5–6 | Wild | Step-relationships, captivity-coded, antihero. |
| 7–8 | Unhinged | Strong taboo, dub-con romantically framed. |
| 9–10 | Off the Rails | Non-con as central trope, extreme taboos. |

## Verified Explicit (R8rly Tier A)

"Verified Explicit" is a **platform certification, not an axis level.** Works carrying high-Spice content (typically Spice 5–6) may apply for R8rly Tier A certification, which verifies the classification, applies platform age-gating, and authorises the "Verified Explicit" designation alongside the R8rly Compass mark. Tier A certification is administered by the R8rly platform and is not part of the open CC BY-SA standard.

## The Axis Independence Principle

Each SHOW axis measures a fully independent dimension of content.

A work can be:

- S0·H5 — No sexual content, maximum romantic intensity
- S6·H0 — Fully saturated sexual content, no romantic emotion
- O5·W0 — Devastating darkness, fully conventional narrative structure
- W10·O0 — Maximum transgression, no emotional darkness

Readers, platforms, and distribution systems read each axis as its own signal. No axis modifies the meaning of any other.

---

## Content Tags

Content tags sit beneath SHOW scores as a secondary reader service layer. Where SHOW axes give intensity levels, content tags name specific content types that readers may wish to know about before engaging with a work.

Tags are applied per work, not per axis. Examples:

on-page grief · captivity dynamics · non-consensual elements · step-romance · on-page violence · suicidal ideation · addiction · forced proximity · morally grey protagonist · dark humour · religious themes · revenge arc · trauma recovery

Content tags are community-sourced and platform-curated. The SHOW Standard defines the axis framework. Tag vocabulary is maintained by the R8rly platform and open for community contribution.

## SHOW and the Standard Family

SHOW is one of three companion standards published by Modern Media Mastery & LMDC:

| Standard | Function | Applies to |
| :-: | :-- | :-- |
| **SHOW** | Content classification | Any finished work, any creation method |
| **VEIL** | Generation authorisation | AI-assisted creation sessions |
| **SCRIPTS** | Experience rating | Reader/viewer/listener experience |

SHOW and VEIL share axis vocabulary. The four SHOW axes (S·H·O·W) map directly into the VEIL generation envelope, so creators working with AI tools move between classification and generation without re-learning any terminology.

SHOW is fully functional as a standalone standard. Creators and publishers with no AI generation workflow use SHOW entirely on its own.

## Applying SHOW

**Who rates a work:** The author or publisher applies an initial SHOW rating on publication. The R8rly community verifies and may adjust ratings through a structured review process. R8rly Verified ratings carry the R8rly Compass mark.

**When to rate:** At publication. Ratings may be revised if content is substantially edited post-publication.

**How to display:**

SHOW S3·H4·O2·W5 — standard inline format

SHOW Verified S3·H4·O2·W5 — R8rly verified format with Compass mark

**Platforms implementing SHOW:** Any platform may implement SHOW ratings under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution required. The R8rly Compass mark and "R8rly Verified" designation remain protected and require platform certification.
